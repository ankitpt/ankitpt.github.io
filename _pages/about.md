I'm Ankit, an Applied Scientist at [Amazon](https://www.amazon.science/) building LLM-powered agents for Customer Service. My work focuses on conversational and voice AI: agents that hold real conversations with customers, resolve their issues end-to-end, and hold up under production traffic. I care about the whole loop, from data and fine-tuning to serving, measurement, and shipping changes that move business metrics.

Before Amazon, I was a Founding ML Engineer at SigIQ.ai, where I developed AI-driven learning experiences for exam preparation. I helped build our flagship product: [PadhAI](https://padhai.ai/), an app for Indian Civil Services exam preparation with over 175,000 users. My work on PadhAI's AI-powered news feature became one of the most engaged-with tools in the app, helping thousands of aspirants connect current events with their exam preparation.

Earlier, I worked on 3D spatial intelligence at [Matterport](https://matterport.com) and on ML for nuclear reactor optimization at [Blue Wave AI Labs](https://bluewaveailabs.com). I have a B.Tech from IIT Kanpur (2018) and an MS from Purdue University (2020). At Purdue I worked with Dr. Ayman Habib in the [Digital Photogrammetry Research Group](https://engineering.purdue.edu/CCE/Academics/Groups/Geomatics/DPRG) on extracting lane markings from LiDAR point clouds, which led to several [publications](/publications/) on deep learning and transfer learning for mapping.

I'm always happy to talk about agents, voice AI, and taking LLM systems from prototype to production. Feel free to reach out on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ankitpt).
